# Style Presets

These are directions, not copy-paste templates.

## Clean

For polished modern menus and social/general experiences.

- restrained surfaces
- strong spacing
- minimal strokes
- simple icons
- clear typography
- subtle motion
- one main accent

Do not turn "clean" into a web SaaS dashboard.

## Cartoon

For brawlers, party games, obbies, and playful action games.

- chunkier silhouettes
- stronger contrast
- expressive but controlled motion
- intentional outlines
- bold icons
- colors pulled from the game world
- tight but readable information density

Do not add UIStroke to everything.

## Competitive

For shooters, sports, ranked games, and fast PvP.

- information-first HUD
- compact controls
- readable numbers
- clear team/status colors
- low visual obstruction
- fast transitions
- minimal decorative animation

## Simulator

For collection games, tycoons, and progression-heavy experiences.

- clear currency/progression hierarchy
- compact top-level HUD
- readable item rarity
- organized shops
- consistent reward presentation
- restrained bright accents

Avoid giant floating bubbles for every action.

## Horror

- minimal HUD
- low visual noise
- controlled contrast
- atmosphere-supporting typography
- rare, deliberate motion

Dark does not mean unreadable.

## Arcade

- bold score/timer hierarchy
- strong feedback
- energetic but short motion
- chunky iconography
- clear round-state transitions

Keep center-screen gameplay clear.

## Futuristic

- precise geometry
- selective glow
- restrained transparency
- data-like hierarchy when appropriate
- clean motion

Do not automatically use purple-blue gradients, glass cards, or neon borders.
